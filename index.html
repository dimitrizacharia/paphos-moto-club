About the Club

Paphos Motorcycle Club is a community of riders who love dirt, tarmac, and everything in between. We organise local rides, training days, hard enduro events, scrambles, and road trips.

Whether you're on a 300 two-stroke, a big adventure bike, or a street machine, there’s a place for you here. Our goal is simple: ride more, ride safer, and have fun together.

  • 🏁 Local races & events
  • 🧭 Adventure rides in Cyprus
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Family-friendly atmosphere
  • 🛠️ Tech & setup tips from members

What we ride

Everything from:

  • KTM / Husqvarna / GASGAS enduro & MX
  • Sherco, Beta & other hard enduro machines
  • Adventure bikes & touring rigs
  • Street bikes & daily commuters
  • Electric bikes (Stark Varg and more)
